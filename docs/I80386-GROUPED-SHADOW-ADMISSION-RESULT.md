# Grouped 386 Windows shadow admission: coverage rises, gate still fails

The [predeclared grouped observer](I80386-GROUPED-SHADOW-ADMISSION.md)
completed an ordinary observer-off / `AT_GROUPED_SHADOW_ADMISSION=1` Windows
pair, 60,000,000 steps per arm, at merged board revision
`80137b0ce78af6a8e65245289995ae5d33737eba`. The full source-bound
reducer reproduced its candidate byte-for-byte, and the
[compact media-neutral receipt](receipts/2026-09-28-i80386-grouped-shadow-admission.json)
was regenerated byte-for-byte with SHA-256
`d326bdf80d33ce88bc2c6564363050df1b0606814fd55569f350db9fa8e799f8`.
Both reports have the same 40-entry source-hash map, verified committed
source bytes, pinned inputs and selected reported guest state. The baseline,
observed report, full candidate, compact candidate, host record and timings
are preserved privately at commit `aa57c96`. The observer does not change
guest execution; observed-arm time is instrumentation overhead, not speed
data.

The grouped grammar admitted **22,624,178** of **55,316,160** eligible
retired ordinals. Only **4,642,606** unique ordinals were in disjoint admitted
runs of at least eight; **4,298,838** were in protected16+VM86. The
predeclared **15M overall / 5M protected16+VM86** opportunity screen failed
on both sides, by 10,357,394 and 701,162 ordinals respectively.

The earlier [form-resolved receipt](I80386-FORM-RESOLVED-ADMISSION-RESULT.md)
had **2,710,535** protected16+VM86 long-run ordinals, so the grouped gain
there is **1,588,303**. The two paired measurements used distinct board
revisions but the same 60M budget and 55,316,160 eligible-retirement
denominator. An admitted-form increase of 1,566,945 can connect previously
short runs, which is why long-run coverage rose by 1,619,201. These are
unique disjoint retired ordinals, not summed local bridge opportunities.

The compact receipt ranks first refusal **reasons** and run-ending reasons,
not exact refused ModR/M forms. In VM86, `deferred-stack-state` accounts for
3,883,402 refused ordinals, `unsafe-code` 2,471,975,
`unsupported-opcode` 2,417,551 and `deferred-control-stack-state` 860,309.
In protected16, the leading reasons are `unsupported-opcode` 1,937,337,
`deferred-stack-state` 867,106 and `deferred-control-stack-state` 339,529.
The physical code, data, event and identity guards remain important even
when syntax is accepted.

The next predeclared **diagnostic** should resolve the remaining refused
forms under the *grouped* grammar, not select another tiny arithmetic slice.
Record first refusal reason, exact prefix/secondary opcode, ModR/M `/reg`,
EA/access shape and both adjacent admitted run lengths for `8E`, stack
push/pop (`50–5F`, `06/07`), direct call/return (`E8`, `C2/C3/CA/CB`),
mixed `FF`, `0F` and `C1`. Continue to classify only completed ordinary
retirements and retain every page, code-write, event, mode and identity cut.
Before any of those forms is admitted to a further grouped replay, focused
ordinary-CPU fixtures must prove segment selector/cache state, precise
stack/fault and RAM effect order, control successors and synchronous device
cuts. A future source-bound off/on 60M replay must recompute **disjoint ≥8
ordinals** by mode and meet the same **15M / 5M** gate; otherwise stop and
revise the grammar. Neither this diagnostic proposal nor the present result
establishes an executable backend or a speedup.
