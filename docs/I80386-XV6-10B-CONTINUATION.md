# Pinned 10B-step stock xv6 board continuation

The [6B-step board receipt](receipts/2026-09-28-xv6-stock-224m-6b-budget.json) records `mem ok`, `fork test OK`, and entry into `bigdir test`, but no `bigdir ok` or final marker. The 6B serial, after its leading form-feed byte, is an exact prefix of the independent pinned QEMU full-suite oracle. The remaining source-ordered `bigdir()`, `uio()` and `exectest()` calls justify a fresh larger ceiling. Ten billion steps leave four billion additional steps beyond the last observed prefix; completion is still not guaranteed.

[`scripts/probe-xv6-usertests-224m-10b-full-suite.py`](../scripts/probe-xv6-usertests-224m-10b-full-suite.py) predeclares the same MIT xv6 revision `eeb7b415dbcb12cc362d0783e41c3d1f44066b17`, stock 224 MiB kernel, boot and filesystem images, free Bochs firmware, exact board execution-source SHA-256 values, `usertests\r` command, and `ALL TESTS PASSED\n$ ` final gate. Its 10,000,000,000-step ceiling is fixed in source. `--preflight` checks pins and source order without running a guest. `--run` requires a clean checkout and a fresh output directory, boots the original stock filesystem, and retains exact live COM1 bytes in an exclusive private file.

The [manual Actions workflow](../.github/workflows/x86-xv6-224m-full-suite.yml) uses that wrapper after rebuilding the pinned public MIT media. It keeps the corrected success gate: a missing final marker or exhausted step budget fails the job, and artifacts upload on either outcome. The 225-minute probe limit leaves upload time within the 240-minute job. No proprietary guest media is used or uploaded.

The underlying probe writes detailed progress to private stderr every 50 million steps. The wrapper emits only `PROGRESS step=<number>` to the Actions log every 500 million steps; it strips the private screen text before printing. Complete stderr, raw report and serial tee remain in the artifact. This coarse heartbeat distinguishes advancing execution from an apparent stall without publishing guest serial content. The workflow has not been dispatched from this proposal.
