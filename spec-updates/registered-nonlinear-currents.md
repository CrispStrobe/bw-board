# Registered nonlinear terminal-current foundation

Status: candidate with focused local qualification; exact-head hosted CI and
Harris remain required before landing.

This foundation permits a registered device to stamp coherent nonlinear terminal currents inside one MNA solve. It does not enable ADP7118 current-limited startup, change its admission, or qualify a vendor waveform model.

`ctx.nonlinearCurrents(key, evaluate)` registers a per-part authority. Its pure `evaluate(read, sourceScale)` callback returns a nonempty `region` string, `currents: Map<terminal, amps>`, `jacobian: Map<terminal, Map<terminal, ampsPerVolt>>`, and positive finite `residualOhms`. Currents are positive out of the part into its net. Every injection row needs a Jacobian map, including an empty map for constant current. Derivative columns may differ from injection rows; all referenced terminals must have authored nets. Missing nets and nonfinite data refuse explicitly.

The evaluator reads the current Newton candidate, never a previous accepted device current. Its region, currents and derivatives describe one coherent local law. Authority keys and terminal-current dimensions must remain constant throughout a solve; a companion's residual scale must remain fixed when evaluating its candidate. The solver owns temporary companions and region history per solve; evaluation must not mutate persistent device state. Nonlinear authorities participate in convergence even when the circuit has no diode or opamp. Final candidates must satisfy the authority's region and residual checks, including after numerical refinement.

Terminal extraction uses the final recorded companions and accumulates ordinary conductance and source contributions. A model's legacy `branchCurrents` hook cannot replace terminal currents owned by this authority. This keeps an instrument's reported current consistent with the actual matrix stamp.

The focused proof registers an authored three-port law with output current `clip((5 - VoutRelativeToGround)/0.05, 0, 0.36)` amperes. It simultaneously draws output current plus `50 uA + 130 uA * min(Iout, 0.2)/0.2` at VIN and returns IQ at GND. A finite 4 ohm supply resistance exposes stale input bookkeeping through the actual supply voltage and source-resistor current. Loads exercise both IQ slopes and the upper clamp; an externally forced output exercises the lower clamp and knee. The forced-output proof qualifies only this generic primitive, not an ADP7118 prebiased-output operating domain.

Tests also cover shifted reference potential, final evaluated region, unchanged live state during bias observation, protected extraction against a deliberately bogus hook, and explicit malformed-authority refusal. A deliberately malformed model uses a test-only closure counter to drop its authority after the first stamp; it must refuse by name. Executable upper- and lower-clamp fixture mutants fail real Board current observations. Five independent production-source mutants omit nonlinear convergence participation, report stale affine intercepts as final currents, permit legacy hooks to overwrite authoritative rows, skip a region check after tiny numerical refinement, or omit restoration of accepted current records after failed refinement. Each is loaded in memory and routed through actual Board callers; the original dispatcher and on-disk source are restored and checked. The refinement control uses a 24.95 ohm load, whose full-scale load line sits at the 0.2 A IQ knee; removing the region check makes the final validator reject the otherwise supported caller. Adjacent unchanged model tests establish that devices which do not register the primitive retain their behavior.

A separate restoration control deliberately uses a discontinuous generic law: output current is 1 pA below 3 V and zero otherwise, with a 100 Tohm load. The full numerical shunt has an admissible approximately 0.990099 V root; removing it leaves no self-consistent root. Failed refinement must restore both that accepted voltage and the matching 1 pA recorded current. Suppressing the companion snapshot fails the real Board observation. This law probes fallback authority only and makes no physical regulator claim.

Key/schema controls additionally reject empty or repeated keys, later key addition/replacement, changing current-row dimensions and changing residual normalization. A restored normal caller still passes after these refusals.

The evaluator owns homotopy scaling: this fixture uses `F_s(v) = s F(v/s)`, scaling target, current ceiling, IQ knee and idle IQ while retaining derivative slopes. The focused controls solve at full source scale and do not claim to exercise the continuation ladder.

Future ADP7118 integration still needs an explicit opt-in, fully bonded duplicate supply/output nets, direct SENSE, absent SS, source/headroom admission, independent piecewise RC transition and capture-integral proofs, transient trial isolation and caller failure semantics. This foundation makes none of those integration claims.
